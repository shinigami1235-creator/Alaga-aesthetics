Questions owners ask first

Is Alaga the lender?

No. Alaga and our financing partner advise you and take your case to lenders and lessors. The loan or lease is with them.

What do I need for the first consultation?

An idea of the investment and its rough cost. Figures and statements come later, through a secure route, once you decide to go ahead.

What happens after the consultation?

You get your options and the advisory fee in writing. Nothing happens until you say yes.

Can you help if my clinic is young?

Yes. A newer clinic needs more of the story on paper, and the consultation tells you what that means for you.

Do you only work with aesthetic clinics?

Medical and aesthetic clinics. The questions are much the same: equipment, rooms, premises and cash flow.
