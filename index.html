Who we are

Alaga is the Tagalog word for care. Dr. Sarah De los Reyes-Bosselaar and Erik Jan Bosselaar founded Alaga Aesthetics in the Netherlands to bring regenerative aesthetic medicine and clinic finance under one name. We work only with registered practitioners, and every conversation starts with listening.
