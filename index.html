Finance notes

Short answers before the long conversation

Eq2 min read

Can your clinic afford that new laser?

Divide the monthly repayment by what one treatment leaves you after costs. If the answer is more treatments than the device can realistically fill in a month, the term or the price needs another look.

Ls2 min read

Leasing or a business loan?

A lease keeps cash in the clinic and often ends with a buy-out. A loan usually costs less in total and the device is yours from day one. Which fits depends on how long you'll use it.

Ex2 min read

Five questions before you expand

Is the current agenda full? What does a new room earn in its first year? Who staffs it? How long is the fit-out? And how much cash do you keep aside while it ramps up?

Is Alaga the lender?

No. Alaga and our financing partner advise you and take your case to lenders and lessors. The loan or lease is with them.

What do I need for the first consultation?

An idea of the investment and its rough cost. Figures and statements come later, through a secure route, once you decide to go ahead.

What happens after the consultation?

You get your options and the advisory fee in writing. Nothing happens until you say yes.

Can you help if my clinic is young?

Yes. A newer clinic needs more of the story on paper, and the consultation tells you what that means for you.

Do you only work with aesthetic clinics?

Medical and aesthetic clinics. The questions are much the same: equipment, rooms, premises and cash flow.
