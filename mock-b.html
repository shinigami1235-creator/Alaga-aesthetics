Who we are

Alaga is the Tagalog word for care. Dr. Sarah De los Reyes-Bosselaar and Erik Jan Bosselaar founded Alaga Aesthetics in the Netherlands to bring regenerative aesthetic medicine and clinic finance under one name.

Sarah brings the clinical side and Erik Jan brings over 25 years of financial advice. We work only with registered practitioners, and every conversation starts with listening.
