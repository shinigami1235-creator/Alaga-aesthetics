    About Dr. Sarah

    Sarah J. De los Reyes Bosselaar, MD, MPH, DPCOM, FPAPSHPI, FAAPAM, MAMEE

    Primary and aesthetic skin health · Occupational medicine and epidemiology

    Sarah is a physician, teacher and researcher in aesthetic skin health and public health. She has practised in Manila for more than 20 years, teaches public health at Ateneo de Manila and De La Salle, and lectures at aesthetic medicine congresses in Asia, Europe, Africa and the Americas. Her published research is in epidemiology and occupational medicine.

    Current appointments

    Sarah heads the Dermatology Section at the Dr. Fe Del Mundo Medical Center in Quezon City, a post she has held since 2022. Since 2023 she has also been the skin health consultant and an IRB member at the Peregrine Eye and Laser Institute. She teaches in two medical schools: as adjunct associate professor at the Ateneo de Manila School of Medicine and Public Health since 2018, and on the Master of Public Health faculty at the De La Salle University Medical and Health Sciences Institute since 2021. In 2026 she co-founded the Association for the Advancement of Physicians in Aesthetic Medicine (AAPAM), and she is the founding president of the Global Stem Cell Academy in the Netherlands.

    Memberships and research

    She has been a fellow of the Philippine Association of Primary Skin Health Physicians since 2003, where she is also international public relations officer, and a diplomate of the Philippine College of Occupational Medicine since 2012. She joined the Dermatologic and Aesthetic Surgery International League in 2015 and the board of the AIDS Society of the Philippines in 2021. In 2024 the Allied Health Society for Asia Pacific named her an international honorary adviser, and since 2025 she has reviewed papers for the Journal of Cosmetic and Regenerative Medicine. She led a University Research Council Big Grant study at Ateneo de Manila University from 2024 to 2025, and before that the COVID-19 costing study in the Philippines for the Ateneo Center for Research and Innovation from 2022 to 2023.

    Congress faculty and awards

    Sarah has taught on the faculty of the Five Continent Congress in Lisbon and Barcelona, the DASIL World Congress in Cape Town, Hanoi, Bangkok and Mexico City, the SIME Congress in Rome, AMSC in Kuala Lumpur and Hong Kong, ASLS in Seoul, the ACE Masterclass at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, ISWAM in Jakarta and PAPSHPI in Manila. She received the Social Responsibility Award from the Medical Skin Health Training Foundation in 2021, the University Scholarly Award from Ateneo de Manila University in 2023 and the Prism Award of Distinction from the Philippine Association of Primary Skin Health Physicians in 2024.
