    About Dr. Sarah

    Sarah J. De los Reyes-Bosselaar, MD, MPH, DPCOM, FPAPSHPI, FAAPAM, MAMEE

    Co-Founder & Medical Director · Primary & Aesthetic Skin Health Physician, Occupational Health Specialist, Medical Educator & Researcher

    Dr. Sarah De los Reyes-Bosselaar is a physician in primary and aesthetic skin health and in occupational medicine, with more than 20 years of clinical practice. She heads the Dermatology Section at the Dr. Fe Del Mundo Medical Center in Metro Manila and teaches as adjunct associate professor at the Ateneo de Manila School of Medicine and Public Health, and her published research covers epidemiology and occupational medicine.

    She lectures on the faculty of international aesthetic medicine congresses, including the Five Continent Congress in Lisbon and Barcelona and the SIME Congress in Rome, and reviews papers for the Journal of Cosmetic and Regenerative Medicine. In 2026 she co-founded the Association for the Advancement of Physicians in Aesthetic Medicine (AAPAM), and she is the founding president of the Global Stem Cell Academy in the Netherlands. At Alaga she leads the Clinical Alliance for partner clinics.
