    About Dr. Sarah

    Sarah J. De los Reyes Bosselaar, MD, MPH, DPCOM, FPAPSHPI, FAAPAM, MAMEE

    Primary and aesthetic skin health · Occupational medicine and epidemiology

    Sarah is a physician, teacher and researcher in aesthetic skin health and public health. She has practised in Manila for more than 20 years, teaches public health at Ateneo de Manila and De La Salle, and lectures at aesthetic medicine congresses in Asia, Europe, Africa and the Americas. Her published research is in epidemiology and occupational medicine.
