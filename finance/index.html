Finance notes

Eq2 min read

Can your clinic afford that new laser?

Divide the monthly repayment by the profit on one treatment. If the device can't do that many treatments a month, look again at the price or the repayment period.

Ls2 min read

Leasing or a business loan?

A lease keeps cash in the clinic and often ends with a buy-out. A loan usually costs less overall and the device is yours from the start. Pick by how long you'll use the device.

Ex2 min read

Questions to ask before you expand

Is your agenda full now? What will the new room earn in its first year? Who will work in it? How long is the fit-out? How much cash do you keep aside while it fills up?

Is Alaga the lender?

No. Alaga and our financing partner advise you and take your case to banks and leasing companies, who give the loan or lease.

What do I need for the first consultation?

The investment you have in mind and roughly what it costs. Figures and statements come later through a secure route if you go ahead.

What happens after the consultation?

You get your options and the advisory fee in writing, and nothing starts until you agree.

Can you help a clinic that opened recently?

Yes. A newer clinic needs more of its plan on paper, and we go through what the bank will ask for in the consultation.

Do you recommend a particular bank?

No. We compare the financing options available and recommend the one that fits your clinic.

Do I still need my accountant?

Yes. We work alongside your accountant or financial adviser.

Who will I deal with?

One contact person from your first question until the financing is signed.
