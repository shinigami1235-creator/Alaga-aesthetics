Skin check

A rough screening from one photo. It reads lines, texture, spots, redness, the skin under the eyes and sagging, then points you to what could help. Everything runs on your phone, and no photo is uploaded.

Start with a front photo

  • Face a window or a soft lamp, so the light is even
  • No makeup, and beauty mode off
  • Hair off the forehead, glasses off

Line your face up with the oval

  • Face in the oval
  • Even light
  • Head straight
  • Hold still
Takes the photo by itself once all four are green
