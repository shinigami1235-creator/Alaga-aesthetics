# Product

Facts below come from the founders' concept deck (4 October 2026) and from Gid's brief for this build. Items marked "inferred" were not confirmed by the founders.

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS and JavaScript hosted on GitHub Pages, with self-hosted fonts and no build step. Chosen by Gid since he hosts all his client sites this way and the founders' IT can take it over without tooling.

## Users

Two audiences on one site.

- Aesthetic practitioners in the Netherlands (BIG-registered doctors and nurses running clinics) who may buy regenerative products and devices, train with Alaga and join its partner network.
- Patients and consumers in the Netherlands looking for skincare guidance and a qualified clinic near them.

## Product Purpose

Alaga Aesthetics by Bosselaar is a new Dutch company founded by Dr. Sarah De los Reyes-Bosselaar and Erik Jan Bosselaar. It plans to distribute regenerative aesthetic products to clinics, train practitioners, and sell home-care cosmeceuticals to patients through those clinics. This first site introduces the company, explains the two paths and starts onboarding partner clinics. Success means a practitioner understands what Alaga offers and registers interest.

## Positioning

"Alaga" is the Filipino (Tagalog) word for care. The founders join a Filipino idea of care with Dutch roots and scientific rigour, and only partner with registered practitioners.

## Operating Context

- Partner clinics are vetted with their KVK registration and the practitioner's BIG number.
- The roadmap starts with legal registration and a "Science of Skin" content library (Q4 2026), then the Clinical Academy portal and distribution (Q1 2027), then the referral network and a symposium.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Basic tier: an introduction site with both paths, the founders' story and a practitioner registration form. Article library, Skin Concierge questionnaire, practitioner portal and webshop are later upgrades.
- Languages: English and Dutch (inferred, since the audience is Dutch).
- No backend yet. Forms open an email to the company (inferred address info@alagaaesthetics.nl, domain not yet bought).
- No KVK number yet, so none is shown.
- No product names, prices, partner names or clinical claims on the site until products are cleared for the EU market.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Alaga Aesthetics by Bosselaar.
- Logo: blue DNA ring with a microscope, a tulip and two leaves around the ALAGA AESTHETICS wordmark in serif capitals. Colour follows the logo's cobalt and navy.
- The deck cover uses Delft-blue engraved illustrations of DNA, cells and microscopes.
- Mood requested by Gid: primarily scientific but still caring, with softer accents for warmth.
- Gid bans: headline plus paragraph plus "Book now" beside a photo as the opening, and arch-shaped image frames.

## Evidence on Hand

- Concept deck text (mission, vision, ecosystem, practitioner and patient offers, questionnaire outlines, roadmap).
- Logo render (WhatsApp image) and the deck cover illustration (ppt/media/image1.png).
- No photos of the founders, no clinic photos, no testimonials, no partner list. Do not invent any of these.

## Product Principles

1. Care first: every claim is about how patients and practitioners are looked after.
2. Science stated plainly: explain what something does without hype.
3. Registered practitioners only.
4. Honest about stage: say what is live now and what comes later.
