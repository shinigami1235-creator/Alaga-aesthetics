# Portraits

Put the two portraits here as `erik.webp` and `sarah.webp`. The home page, the finance page and the clinic page pick them up without any code change. Until a file is here, its card shows the engraved panel with the initials.

- Size: 4:5 portrait, at least 1200 × 1500 px, under 250 KB each.
- Framing: three-quarter length or waist up, head in the top third and the person centred, since phones crop the sides to a narrower 3:5 card. Keep the bottom quarter plain, since the name sits over it.
- Background: plain and light, or a soft studio colour.
- Pose: standing power pose. Erik Jan stands on the left of the page, so he turns slightly to his left (towards the centre). Dr. Sarah stands on the right, so she turns slightly to her right.
