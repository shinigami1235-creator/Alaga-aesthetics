What partner clinics get

For BIG-registered doctors and nurses running a clinic in the Netherlands. Partner clinics are checked with their KVK registration and BIG number.

  1. Regenerative products

    Biostimulators, threads, fillers and devices for partner clinics.

  2. Alaga Academy

    Training and certification in regenerative treatments.

  3. Device support

    Technical support, white papers and help when a device gives trouble.

  4. Patient referrals

    Patients from our national skin triage are sent to partner clinics near them.
